Overview

One hundred and one diffraction patterns from 900 to 1300 °C, kept as they came and made legible.

The measurements Irene Urcelay-Olabarria proposed and took with Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón at ESRF beamline ID22 (proposal HC-6528, sample 400_4), treated here by Josu M. Igartua so that the team can interpret them: a heating ramp in 25 °C steps, then time series at 1240–1300 °C with a scan every 4.4 minutes, 2.8 hours of them at 1270 °C, and an empty-capillary background; 10.6 hours of beamtime on 10–11 June 2026. The files arrived as bare three-column tables with every piece of metadata in their names. This site records how they were read, what was recovered from them, how the copies were named, what the numbers and the projections already show (broad maxima from 1000 °C, sharp reflections growing from the 1270 °C hold), and what the group can do next. The originals are untouched; every copy is checksummed against them on every build.

The extractionFrom an unreadable folder to a named, verified, described dataset

The extracted folder had no read permission on any file. Once readable, the files turned out to carry nothing but numbers: 2θ in 0.001° steps to 27°, an intensity and its error. Everything else was recovered from the names and the file system: the order and the clock time of every scan (the modification times agree with the elapsed seconds to the second), the nominal temperature, and from the contents two exposure classes, a common intensity scale, and one anomalous scan. The copies in renamed/ are named so that a listing reads as the experiment: sequence, temperature, elapsed time, class.

How this site works

Method →
01

The originals are never edited

originals/ holds the 101 files as received (one change: read permission). renamed/ holds one copy each; tools/analyze.py re-checks every SHA-256 on every build and stops if a byte differs.

02

Every step is a protocol entry

The Protocol is a data file: one entry per step with the command run and the evidence returned. It is the report to the group of how the data were extracted.

03

Facts, ideas and questions are kept apart

A finding is a statement about the files with its numbers. An idea is what to do with them, with a status. A question is what only Irene or the beamline can answer. A decision is Josu's.

04

Everything is logged

The log has one dated entry per event, ids that never change, and is regenerated with every page from data/*.yml. The roadmap says what phase the project is in.

    What this is

    The working site of the ESRF ID22 measurements proposed by Irene Urcelay-Olabarria (proposal HC-6528, sample 400_4), taken by Irene, Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón: 101 powder diffraction scans from 900 to 1300 °C kept exactly as received, copied under names that say what they are, the figures of their evolution, and everything the team needs to interpret them, logged step by step. Josu M. Igartua treats the data.
