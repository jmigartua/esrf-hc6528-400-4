L0142026-10-23finding

Figure ids that differ only by case collide on the Mac's file system: five maps overwrote five others

The maps were first named mapT- (temperature) and mapt- (time). drs-publish built all fifteen without a word, but the folder held ten: on the Mac's case-insensitive file system mapt-full.pdf is mapT-full.pdf, and the five time maps, built last, had replaced the five temperature maps under the temperature maps' names. Nothing in the build said so; the manifest listed what it had written. Renamed map-temp-, map-time-, map-hold-* and rebuilt. To be carried to diff-ref-struct: a publication set with two ids equal up to case should be refused (the file dictionary's names are case-insensitive on the platform the app is built for).

L0132026-10-23finding

The core panics on reading its own rebinned .xye output (capacity overflow in the FullProf reader)

drs data op <scan>.xye rebin step=0.01 mode=average --out <out>.xye writes a three-column file with a # header ("# pattern (written by drs 0.75.0)", "# wavelength not stated", …) and σ = 1e30 on the points without weight. Reading that file back, by drs data identify, drs plots open or a plot set, panics in the registry's FullProf reader with capacity overflow (fullprof.rs:854 with the header, :641 without it). The same data written as XYDATA (--out <out>.xys) are read without trouble, so the maps use .xys (L012). The original .xye files, with no header and ordinary σ, are read fine; the trigger is in the rebinned file's text (the 1e30 σ or the long decimals), not checked further here. To be carried to diff-ref-struct as a request, with one of the files in drs/Rebinned/ as the case; a reader should refuse a file, never panic.

drs 0.75.0 · core/src/formats/fullprof.rs:641 and :854 · for the diff-ref-struct project (plots/REQUESTS.md or app/REQUESTS.md)
L0122026-10-23work

The maps: the intensity on the temperature–2θ and time–2θ planes, from the scans rebinned by the core

Josu: also the projections onto the plane temperature against 2θ. That is drs-publish's map view (one row per scan at its value of the variable, 2θ along, intensity as colour). On the files' 0.001° grid it was too heavy for LaTeX (L009: a 364 MB PGF), so the scans were first rebinned by the core, drs data op <file> rebin step=… mode=average --out …, into drs/Rebinned/ as XYDATA files (.xys): 0.01° for the whole pattern (2700 columns), 0.005° for the four windows; 200 files, each a pure average of the measured points, the measured files untouched.

Three series, as for the projections, in six more plot sets (one per grid): one scan per nominal temperature against T (25 rows, 900 to 1300 °C: the long scan at each temperature where there is one, else the last scan of its series; the true temperature–2θ plane); all 100 scans against time (the temperature repeats 39 times at 1270 °C, so time is the axis and the caption gives the programme); the 1270 °C hold against minutes into it. The colour scale is the square root of the intensity (map_scale = "sqrt" in the sets), so that the weak broad bands of the first stage are seen beside the sharp lines of the second; linear hid them (the first build, L009). Fifteen maps in a second publication set, esrf-maps.drp, with written captions; the Figures page shows them under the projections, and build.py now reads the captions of both sets.

L0092026-10-23decision

The figures through diff-ref-struct: the core's plot sets and drs-publish's projections, a drs/ project folder

Josu: a good case to start probing the app diff-ref-struct, its Plots module, for now; a set of figures that show how the diffractograms evolve, the projection plot for the complete diffractograms and for smaller 2θ ranges, each with a comprehensive caption; and later the app itself in projection mode, to see more detail.

Decided:

  • The figures are made by the app's own pipeline, never by a script of this project: the core drs (0.75.0) reads the .xye files (its registry recognises them as xye), opens them as plot sets and computes the projections with hidden lines (plots::project); drs-publish (0.34.0) typesets them (F3, view = "projection"), every character by LuaLaTeX in Latin Modern, preset upvehu-phd at the full text width.
  • A project folder for the app, drs/: Data/ is a link to renamed/ (no second copy of 112 MB), Plots/ holds the plot sets, Publish/ the publication set and its build. Josu opens any .drv in the app's Plots module and switches to the Projection view (I12).
  • Six plot sets, because a set has one displacement and the whole pattern and a 3° window need different steps: the 19 long scans against T in °C (ramp-long, step 0.3° and 120 counts; ramp-long-w, 0.03° for the windows); all 100 scans against time in hours since scan 001 (all-scans, 0.06° and 30 counts; all-scans-w, 0.004° and 25); the 39 scans of the 1270 °C hold against minutes into it (hold-1270, 0.15° and 25; hold-1270-w, 0.008° and 25). The variable of every member is typed with its source (the nominal set point, or the elapsed seconds of the scan's name), since the files say nothing the core could read.
  • Four windows for the smaller ranges, chosen from the peak lists of scans 005, 032, 070 and 100: 5.4–6.2° (the strongest growing reflection, 5.79°, alone), 7–10° (the first stage's broad maxima and three sharp reflections of the second), 11.3–13° and 14–15°; and the whole pattern from 0.5° (the beamstop region left out).
  • The captions are written in the publication set and replace drs-publish's defaults; the site reads them from there, so the figure and its caption have one source.
  • A map of the 100 scans was built once and dropped: on the files' 0.001° grid its PGF is 364 MB and its PDF 12.7 MB, and the set's preview never compiled. The map is the app's Map view, drawn as pixels (I12).

Set aside: matplotlib figures from this project's own script (Josu asked for the app; the ThesisFigures rules are the app's too); the clock time or the temperature of every scan as the variable of the 100-scan sets (time is unique and monotonic, the temperature repeats 39 times).

L0032026-10-23finding

What the 101 files are: headerless 2θ–intensity–esd tables, two exposure classes, 10.6 hours of experiment

Read file by file:

  • three columns, no header, step 0.001° in every file, 2θ to 26.999°; twenty files start at 0.008° (26 992 points), eighty-one at 0.009° (26 991 points) (F01);
  • the two lengths are two exposure classes, median relative error 0.040 against 0.047 above 2°; every C-tagged original is in the long class, three bare ones too (F02);
  • the integrated intensity is the same for both classes, 4.5–4.7 million counts, 4.2 million for the background: normalised by the reduction (F03);
  • modification time minus the elapsed seconds in the name is 2026-06-10 18:09:48 for all 101 files: the clock time of every scan is known (F06);
  • a crude peak count shows a crystalline phase appearing between 975 and 1000 °C and a second growth of crystallinity from the 1270 °C hold to 1300 °C (F04);
  • scan 085 (bare 1290) has a relative error of 0.066, far above its class (F05);
  • the beamstop shadow ends at about 0.02° (F08). The experiment: a 25 °C-step ramp 900 → 1225 °C with one long scan per step, then short scans every 262 s at 1240, 1245, 1250, 1270 (39 scans, 2.8 h), 1275, 1280 and 1300 °C, two more long scans at 1290 and 1300 °C, and an empty-capillary background; 18:09 on 10 June to 04:46 on 11 June 2026 (Timeline).
