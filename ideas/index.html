Ideas

What to do with the data

Ideas I01–I11 for the analysis, each with its status (idea, planned, doing, done, declined), what it needs, and the finding it comes from. An idea moves only with a log entry. The first, plotting, is the next phase and waits for Josu's word (B02); the rest are sketched so the group can order them.

I01 Plot the contents: every scan, then the series as a waterfall and as a map done

Phase 1 · done as projections through diff-ref-struct (L009, L010); the map stays in the app (I12)

Three views, all from the stacked matrix (F01), 2θ above 0.03° (F08):

  • every scan on its own, intensity against 2θ, with the esd band, named by its new name;
  • the series as a waterfall (offset stack) ordered by sequence, colour by temperature, so the appearance near 1000 °C and the growth from 1270 °C are seen at a glance;
  • a 2θ–time map (heat map, scans as rows, log colour scale), with the temperature as a second axis on the right. Thesis-figure rules (ThesisFigures skill): LaTeX text, one canvas size for the series, one colour per temperature, a shared intensity axis where the plots are compared.
B02 · Roadmap, phase 1

I02 Kinetics at 1270 °C from the 39-scan hold idea

Phase 3 · needs I01, I03, a peak list

The 1270 °C hold is 39 scans at 262 s over 2.8 h with the peak count growing 54 → 138 (F04). Integrate a few well-separated growing peaks against time, fit an Avrami or a simple growth law, and compare with the 1275, 1280 and 1300 °C series (8, 4, 13 scans) for an activation energy if the same peaks are followed. The crude peak count is not the observable; integrated intensities of named reflections are.

F04 · S07

I03 Subtract the empty-capillary background idea

Phase 2 · needs a scale factor

Scan 101 is the capillary and furnace alone (F03). Subtract it from every sample scan with a scale factor fitted in a peak-free window (the sample absorbs part of the capillary's scattering, so the factor is below 1), and check the residual is flat where no peaks are. The flat part that remains is the sample's diffuse scattering: in the 900–950 °C scans that is nearly the whole signal (F04), and its shape is information about the amorphous or molten state.

F03 · S15

I04 Convert 2θ to d and Q once the wavelength is known idea

blocked by Q01

With 2θ to 27° at high resolution the wavelength is short (ID22 works near 0.35 Å, which would give d down to 0.75 Å). Until the wavelength is confirmed (Q01) nothing can be indexed; once it is, add d and Q columns to the stacked matrix and label the plots in both.

Q01

I05 Identify the phases idea

Phase 3 · needs I03, I04

From the background-subtracted long scans (one per temperature step, best statistics): a peak list at 1000 °C (the first phase) and at 1300 °C (the first plus the second), then a search against the databases the group uses, constrained by what the sample is (Q02). The 2θ positions of the peaks that appear near 1000 °C and of those that appear from 1270 °C are two separate lists.

F04 · Q02

I06 Lattice parameters against temperature idea

Phase 4 · needs I05

Once a phase is indexed, a Le Bail or Pawley fit of every scan gives its cell against temperature (thermal expansion along the ramp) and against time (at the holds). Peak shifts during the 1270 °C hold would show whether the composition of the first phase changes while the second grows.

I05

I07 Sequential Rietveld refinement of the whole series idea

Phase 4 · needs I05, I06 · the link to rietveld-minimisation

With structures for the phases, a sequential refinement over the 100 sample scans gives phase fractions against time and temperature, the quantitative form of F04. The series is a good test case for the robust-path methods of the rietveld-minimisation project (101 refinements from one start).

I05, I06 · rietveld-minimisation

I08 Difference patterns between consecutive scans idea

Phase 2 · cheap, after I01

Scan n minus scan n−1 (same class, after I03) isolates what changed in 262 s: positive peaks growing, negative peaks vanishing. Over the 1270 °C hold this shows directly whether the second growth is new reflections or the sharpening of existing ones.

F04

I09 Treat the exposure classes separately where statistics matter idea

a rule for I02, I06, I07

Long and short scans have different counting times (F02) and the normalisation hides it (F03). Any fit that uses the esd column is fine (the esd carries the difference); any statistic that counts points above a threshold, as the crude peak count does, is biased between the classes and should be done within a class, or with the threshold scaled.

F02, F03

I10 A browser explorer of the 101 scans idea

Phase 1 or 2 · as sand-emissivity's spectrum explorer

A page on this site that loads a down-sampled copy of the matrix (every 10th point is 2700 points per scan) and lets the group pick scans by temperature or sequence, overlay them, zoom, and read 2θ and intensity under the cursor. The sand-emissivity site has the pattern (assets/scripts/data-viewer.js); the full-resolution files stay in renamed/.

I11 Ask ESRF for the raw and reduction metadata idea

with Irene · answers Q01, Q04, Q09

The .xye files are the end of the beamline's reduction. The ESRF data portal keeps the raw frames and the reduction parameters (wavelength, the meaning of w0003, counting time per scan, the thermocouple log) under the proposal HC-6528. One request answers three questions at once.

Q01, Q04, Q06, Q09

I12 Work in the app's Plots module, projection view, on the six plot sets planned

for Josu · drs/Plots/*.drv in /Applications/diff-ref-struct.app

The six sets of L009 open in the app (File ▸ Open Plot Set…, or a double-click on a .drv with drs/ as the project): the Projection tab draws them with the same core function as the figures, and the displacement, the order, the dimming and the windows are changed live; the Map tab draws the 100-scan map that was too heavy for LaTeX (L009). Zoom, cursor and selection are linked between views. What is found there goes to the log as findings, and a new figure is one more [[figure]] in drs/Publish/esrf-series.drp.

L009 · app/manual/plots.html of diff-ref-struct

I13 Benchmark the family's sessions the same way, with cost idea

after L011

tools/timing.py measures this project's sessions from the transcripts. The same tool, pointed at the other family projects' transcripts, would say how long a site of this form takes the second and third time; the token counts it already reads give the cost at the model's prices; and a log entry that names its task (T1, T2, …) joins the log to the benchmark. A queued message marks where Josu changed the task while it ran, which is itself a measure of how much the brief moved.

On this page

    What this is

    The working site of the ESRF ID22 measurements proposed by Irene Urcelay-Olabarria (proposal HC-6528, sample 400_4), taken by Irene, Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón: 101 powder diffraction scans from 900 to 1300 °C kept exactly as received, copied under names that say what they are, the figures of their evolution, and everything the team needs to interpret them, logged step by step. Josu M. Igartua treats the data.
