The experiment's stages

The 101 scans read as an experiment: fifteen stages S01–S15 from the heating ramp to the background, with the clock times (10–11 June 2026), the scan cadence, and the crude peak count and strongest peak above 1° at the start and end of each stage. The stages are drawn from the names (temperature, elapsed time) and from the files (exposure class, statistics); the counts are recomputed from the files on every build.

  • S01 · scans 001–014 · 18:09–21:05Heating ramp, 900 → 1225 °C in 25 °C steps

    Fourteen long scans, one per temperature step, all tagged with a C in the original names. The sample is almost featureless at 900–950 °C; a crystalline phase appears between 975 and 1000 °C (F04).

    14 scans · one long scan every ~810 s (13.5 min) · peaks above 1°: 2 → 39 · max I above 1°: 612 → 621

  • S02 · scans 015–017 · 21:15–21:36Transition to time-resolved mode, 1225 → 1240 °C

    Three long scans without the C tag: one more at 1225 °C, two at 1240 °C. Same exposure class as the ramp scans, but spaced like the series that follows.

    3 scans · long scans ~620–640 s apart · peaks above 1°: 53 → 63 · max I above 1°: 603 → 625

  • S03 · scans 018–022 · 21:41–21:581240 °C series

    The first short scans. From here to scan 083 the cadence is one scan every 4.4 min.

    5 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 42 → 51 · max I above 1°: 681 → 652

  • S04 · scans 023–025 · 22:03–22:111245 °C series

    Three scans.

    3 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 48 → 52 · max I above 1°: 644 → 633

  • S05 · scans 026–030 · 22:16–22:331250 °C series

    Five scans.

    5 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 57 → 56 · max I above 1°: 663 → 716

  • S06 · scans 031 · 22:40–22:401266 °C, a single scan

    One scan on the way to 1270 °C.

    1 scan · 436 s after the previous scan · peaks above 1°: 58 · max I above 1°: 636

  • S07 · scans 032–070 · 22:45–01:311270 °C isothermal hold, 39 scans over 2.8 h

    The longest series. The peak count grows steadily from 54 to 138 and the strongest peak from 630 to 990 counts: something is forming or growing at constant nominal temperature. The natural dataset for kinetics.

    39 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 54 → 138 · max I above 1°: 632 → 986

  • S08 · scans 071 · 01:36–01:361272 °C, a single scan

    One scan at an intermediate set point.

    1 scan · 290 s after the previous scan · peaks above 1°: 142 · max I above 1°: 992

  • S09 · scans 072–079 · 01:40–02:111275 °C series

    Eight scans; the growth continues.

    8 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 146 → 190 · max I above 1°: 1026 → 1244

  • S10 · scans 080–083 · 02:15–02:281280 °C series

    Four scans.

    4 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 178 → 210 · max I above 1°: 1269 → 1480

  • S11 · scans 084–085 · 02:41–02:561290 °C: one long scan, then one anomalous short scan

    Scan 084 is a long scan tagged 1290C. Scan 085, tagged 1290, has a median relative error of 0.066 against 0.046–0.049 for every other short scan, and its peak count drops from 227 to 160: a shorter or partial exposure (F05).

    2 scans · 776 s, then 904 s · peaks above 1°: 227 → 160 · max I above 1°: 1422 → 1689

  • S12 · scans 086 · 03:04–03:041299 °C, a single scan

    One scan on the way to 1300 °C.

    1 scan · 458 s after the previous scan · peaks above 1°: 228 · max I above 1°: 1779

  • S13 · scans 087–099 · 03:08–04:011300 °C series, 13 scans

    The peak count reaches about 300 and the strongest peak 2600 counts.

    13 scans · short scans every ~262 s · peaks above 1°: 230 → 293 · max I above 1°: 1808 → 2634

  • S14 · scans 100 · 04:11–04:111300 °C, a closing long scan

    Tagged 1300C in the original: the long-exposure counterpart of the series, 322 peaks.

    1 scan · 638 s after the previous scan · peaks above 1°: 322 · max I above 1°: 2613

  • S15 · scans 101 · 04:46–04:46Background: empty capillary, no sample

    A long scan with no sample, 0 peaks above 1°, integrated intensity 4.16 million against 4.5–4.7 million with the sample. The reference for background subtraction.

    1 scan · 2064 s after the previous scan · peaks above 1°: 0 · max I above 1°: 675

Reading the timeline

  • Two cadences. Long scans 620–900 s apart (the ramp, the transitions, the two closing long scans); short scans 250–290 s apart (the series). The cadence is the scan macro's; the classes are told apart by the contents (F02).
  • Two events in the sample. A crystalline phase appears between 975 and 1000 °C (S01) and stays roughly constant to 1250 °C; a second growth of crystallinity begins during the 1270 °C hold (S07) and continues to 1300 °C (S13, S14). See F04.
  • One anomaly. Scan 085 in S11 (F05).
  • The temperatures are set points typed into the scan names, not a thermocouple record (F07).
On this page

    What this is

    The ESRF ID22 measurements proposed by Irene Urcelay-Olabarria (proposal HC-6528, sample 400_4) and taken by Irene, Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón: 101 powder diffraction scans from 900 to 1300 °C kept exactly as received, copied under names that say what they are, the figures of their evolution, and what the files show. Josu M. Igartua treats the data so that the team can interpret them.

    Irene Urcelay-Olabarria (proposer) · Iñigo González de Arrieta & Mireia Sainz-Menchón (experimental team, with Irene) · Josu M. Igartua (data treatment)
