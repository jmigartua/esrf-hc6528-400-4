The 101 files

What is in originals/ and renamed/: the format, the fields of the original names, the scheme of the new ones, and one row per scan with the facts computed from its contents. The table is computed from the files on every build; the files themselves are not on the site.

Instrument
ESRF beamline ID22, high-resolution powder diffraction (Josu, 23 October 2026); the reduced .xye files of proposal HC-6528, measured 10–11 June 2026
Team
Irene Urcelay-Olabarria, the proposer; Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón, the experimental team with her; Josu M. Igartua, the treatment of the data for their interpretation
Files
101 .xye scans: 100 with the sample, 1 empty-capillary background
Columns
2θ (degrees), intensity, estimated standard deviation; no header line in any file
Grid
Step 0.001° in every file (min = max = 0.001); 2θ from 0.008° (20 long scans) or 0.009° (81 short scans) to 26.999°
Classes
20 long (26992 points, median e/y ≈ 0.040) and 81 short (26991 points, median e/y ≈ 0.047); every one of the 16 C-tagged originals is long
Temperatures
25 nominal values from 900 to 1300 °C
Clock
Scan 001 at 2026-06-10 18:09:48; last background at +38192 s (2026-06-11 04:46:20); 10.6 h in all
Integrity
Every copy in renamed/ is byte-identical to its original (SHA-256, checked on every build); modification times preserved

File format

Plain text, extension .xye, no header, three whitespace-separated columns per line:

2theta_deg   intensity   esd
    0.008000  0.0000000      1567.0943
    0.023000  1690.1763      412.41486
   26.999000  82.555925      3.5181567
  • 2θ in degrees, step 0.001° in every file, from 0.008° or 0.009° to 26.999°.
  • Intensity in counts after the beamline's reduction; the integral over the whole range is the same for every sample scan (F03), so the reduction normalised them.
  • esd, the estimated standard deviation of the intensity. Zero-intensity points at the start have a large esd: the beamstop's shadow, to about 0.02° (F08).
  • The only differences between files in length are one point: 20 files begin at 0.008°, 81 at 0.009° (F01, F02).

The original names

hc6528_400_4_Qu_<tag>_w0003_b0001-<N>s.xye
hc6528
The ESRF proposal, HC-6528.
400_4
The sample.
Qu
A sample label; meaning to be confirmed.
<tag>
The nominal temperature as typed at the beamline, either with a C (900C … 1300C) or bare (1225 … 1300), or background_nosample. Not unique: 39 files share 1270.
w0003
A reduction parameter, constant over the set, not identified from the files.
b0001
The bin width, 0.001°, which the data's step confirms.
<N>s
Seconds elapsed since the first scan. The file's modification time is 2026-06-10 18:09:48 + N for all 101 files (F06).

The naming scheme of the copies

hc6528_400_4_Qu_<seq>_T<temp>C_t<elapsed>s_<class>.xye
<seq>
001–101, the acquisition order, so that an alphabetical listing is the experiment in order.
T<temp>C
The nominal temperature, zero-padded to four digits: T0900C, T1270C. The background has background_nosample here.
t<elapsed>s
The elapsed seconds of the original, zero-padded to five digits: t00000s, t16530s.
<class>
long (26992 points from 0.008°, median e/y ≈ 0.040) or short (26991 points from 0.009°, ≈ 0.047), read from the contents (F02).

Example: hc6528_400_4_Qu_1270_w0003_b0001-16530s.xye → hc6528_400_4_Qu_032_T1270C_t16530s_short.xye. The constant fields w0003_b0001 and the C suffix are not in the new names; both are in file_index.csv and in the table below. Why this scheme and what was set aside: P09.

    What this is

    The ESRF ID22 measurements proposed by Irene Urcelay-Olabarria (proposal HC-6528, sample 400_4) and taken by Irene, Iñigo González de Arrieta and Mireia Sainz-Menchón: 101 powder diffraction scans from 900 to 1300 °C kept exactly as received, copied under names that say what they are, the figures of their evolution, and what the files show. Josu M. Igartua treats the data so that the team can interpret them.
